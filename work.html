Work

These are the products I’ve helped build for other people, and the part I played in each.

Minimalister

Voicenotes.com — an AI-powered voice recording and note-taking app that uses the latest models, such as ChatGPT and Claude, to record and transcribe speech and turn it into structured notes. I integrated Firebase for real-time, server-driven data over WebSockets, and built the app with Nuxt 3, Laravel, and a self-hosted RAG model.

BuyMeACoffee.com — I led frontend development for key features, including payments, memberships, and e-commerce workflows. Working closely with the design and backend teams, I helped ship features used by millions of creators around the world.

Bio.link — I developed a single-page application that lets creators build customizable link pages, with custom domains and email subscriptions. I designed and built its interface with the creator’s experience at the center.

Other work

Arclif.com — a social network for architects. I built it with Vue.js and Node.js, integrated the backend services, and extended it to mobile with React Native.
